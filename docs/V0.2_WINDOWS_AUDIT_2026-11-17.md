## Code-level analysis (hypothesis, not a proven ID mapping)

In `src/content.js`, history export chooses
`item.conversation_id || item.id` when computing the incremental key.
Shared items are listed via `/backend-api/shared_conversations`, and a
shared-only entry may carry a **share ID** in `item.id`. During export,
`exportConversation` can instead use a resolved conversation object whose
`conversation_id` becomes the saved `metadata.json` conversation ID.
This can produce an incremental-key identity different from an exported
conversation ID. **It has not been proven that this explains the four specific
local mismatch IDs.**
